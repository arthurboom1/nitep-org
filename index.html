How pooling works

Small organizations, combined purchasing power.

Vendors price security services by volume — which has always meant small nonprofits pay the most per device for the same protection. NITEP fixes the math.

STEP 1

Verify your 501(c)(3)

Submit a short application with your EIN and device count. Any mission qualifies — health, housing, education, arts, faith-based, and beyond.

STEP 2

Join the pool

Your endpoints are counted alongside every other member organization's. The combined total is what sets the rate — not your size alone.

STEP 3

Pay the pooled rate

Your organization receives 20% off Boom 365: MSSP Pro managed security and managed cloud server hosting, automatically — plus 20% or more off published BoomTalk™ VoIP rates and special program pricing on BoomTalk™ Live.

Questions

What nonprofit leaders ask us.

Is the 20% discount really automatic?

Yes. If your 501(c)(3) status is verified and you meet the 20-endpoint minimum, the discount applies from day one. There is no negotiation and no annual re-qualification.

What exactly does the discount apply to?

The 20% NITEP rate applies to Boom 365: MSSP Pro, Boom Logic's managed security service plan, and to managed cloud server hosting. Members also receive 20% or more off published BoomTalk™ VoIP rates on every plan, plus special program pricing on BoomTalk™ Live. Exact pricing depends on your device count — the pricing calculator on boomlogic.com shows your NITEP rate instantly.

We're a small clinic. Do we count?

If you have at least 20 devices — workstations, laptops, servers — you qualify. That's the point of the pool: your 25 endpoints get priced like a member of a much larger whole.

What happens as the pool grows?

The pool is the program's engine. As more organizations join, the collective purchasing position strengthens — which is why NITEP's discount is structural rather than promotional.

Who runs NITEP?

NITEP is operated by Boom Logic (The Boom Company, Inc.), a Los Angeles MSP/MSSP providing managed IT services and cybersecurity for nonprofits and SMBs since 2008, in partnership with founding member All For Health, Health For All.
